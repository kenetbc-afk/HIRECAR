How It Works

Our 5-Step Process

A clear, repeatable cycle designed to systematically address every disputable item on your reports.

1

Intake Call

We review your current credit reports (soft pull only — FCRA § 604), identify disputable items, and build your case file.

2

Document Preparation

Our team prepares customized dispute letters, validation requests, and any supporting documents tailored to your specific accounts.

3

Bureau Disputes Filed

Prepared documents are submitted to the credit bureaus and/or creditors on your behalf via certified mail with tracking.

4

Response Window

Bureaus have 30–45 days to investigate and respond. We monitor progress and prepare follow-up documents as needed.

5

Next Cycle

We review results, update your case file, and begin the next round of disputes targeting remaining items until your goals are met.
